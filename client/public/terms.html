02 What RPS Royale is

RPS Royale is a free, single-elimination rock-paper-scissors tournament for 2–4 players. It's a hobby project, not a commercial product: there's nothing to buy, no ads, and no subscriptions.

03 Access & identity

There's no account system. When you play through Discord, the game asks Discord for your display name so it can show it in the arena instead of asking you to type one. On the web, you just type a name yourself. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what that involves.

04 Acceptable use

Play fair. Specifically, don't:

  • Exploit bugs to force wins, crash a game in progress, or lock other players out
  • Automate or script your moves
  • Harass, threaten, or abuse other players through the game or its chat

Breaking these is grounds for us cutting off your access, per Termination below.
